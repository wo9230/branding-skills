# Excluded Paths

The extraction process excludes these path classes:

```text
.git/
.env
.env.*
node_modules/
.venv/
dist/
data/
customers/
exports/
__pycache__/
*.pem
*.key
*.csv
*.xlsx
*.sql
*.dump
```

Files outside the approved public manifest are not copied. Unapproved source material and internal records are intentionally not enumerated here.
